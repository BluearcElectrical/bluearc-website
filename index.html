About Blue Arc Electrical

Blue Arc Electrical Limited is an industrial electrical contractor servicing Palmerston North, Manawatū and the lower North Island.

We specialise in industrial automation, PLC programming, industrial maintenance and electrical breakdown support, working alongside manufacturers and industrial businesses to minimise downtime and improve productivity.
